The maker

Work that looks you in the eye.

Lucky’s Art & Crafts grew out of a stall and a love of animals — lions, owls, turtles, guinea fowl — rendered in paint, wood and recycled metal.

Edward Chekasondo runs the stall and the WhatsApp line. Follow the work on X @LuckyArtCrafts.
